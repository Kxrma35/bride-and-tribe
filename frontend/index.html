The Bride & Tribe Story

Four Sisters.
One Unbreakable
Promise.

In 2016, Abigael, Beverlyn, Belinda, and June pooled their savings, their skills, and their shared obsession with bridal fashion to open a single-room boutique in Nairobi's Karen neighborhood. No investors. No safety net. Just four sisters who refused to quit on each other.

What began as a dream became a destination — where brides from across East Africa come not just for a dress, but for the kind of care that only a family business can give. To this day, every appointment is handled by one of the sisters personally.

“We built this for each other first, and for you second — because love is what makes a business last.”

— The Four Sisters, Founders of Bride & Tribe

Sourced from Around the World

Curated Globally.
Cherished Forever.

Our founders travel the world to handpick every gown in our collection — from the fashion capitals of Europe to the finest ateliers across the globe. They inspect each seam, test every fabric for its drape and weight, and reject anything that doesn't meet the Bride & Tribe standard.

We believe the most beautiful gowns have no single origin — they come from the best designers the world has to offer. The result is a collection as diverse and extraordinary as the brides who wear them.
